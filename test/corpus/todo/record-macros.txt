These include templates require macro substitution. The :error markers record
deferred support for record operands and statement fragments in raw source.
Since {N} reads as a name, a bare {N} that prefixes another statement on the
same line (for instance one expanding to a modifier) now starts that statement
instead of being skipped as an extra.
