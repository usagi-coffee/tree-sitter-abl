================================================================================
CATCH and FINALLY - empty and populated bodies remain visible
================================================================================

CATCH e AS MyError: END CATCH.
CATCH e AS MyError: MESSAGE "caught". END CATCH.
FINALLY: END FINALLY.
FINALLY: MESSAGE "done". END FINALLY.
