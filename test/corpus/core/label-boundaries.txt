================================================================================
Labels - keyword prefixes, abbreviations and FOR blocks
================================================================================

do-loop: DO: LEAVE do-loop. END.
repeat-later: REPEAT: LEAVE repeat-later. END.
finally2: DO: LEAVE finally2. END.
proc: REPEAT: LEAVE proc. END.
CHOOSE: FOR EACH tt_label_check: LEAVE CHOOSE. END.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

(source_code
  (do_statement label: (identifier) (body (leave_statement label: (identifier))))
  (repeat_statement label: (identifier) (body (leave_statement label: (identifier))))
  (do_statement label: (identifier) (body (leave_statement label: (identifier))))
  (repeat_statement label: (identifier) (body (leave_statement label: (identifier))))
  (for_statement label: (identifier)
    (record_phrase record: (identifier))
    (body (leave_statement label: (identifier)))))

================================================================================
Labels - control flow and error handling
================================================================================

CHOOSE: REPEAT ON ERROR UNDO CHOOSE, RETRY CHOOSE: NEXT CHOOSE. END.
FUNCTION: REPEAT ON ENDKEY UNDO FUNCTION, LEAVE FUNCTION: UNDO FUNCTION, NEXT FUNCTION. END.
PROCEDURE: REPEAT: UNDO PROCEDURE, RETRY PROCEDURE. END.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

(source_code
  (repeat_statement label: (identifier)
    (on_error_phrase undo_label: (identifier) retry_label: (identifier))
    (body (next_statement label: (identifier))))
  (repeat_statement label: (identifier)
    (on_endkey_phrase undo_label: (identifier) leave_label: (identifier))
    (body (undo_statement undo_label: (identifier) next_label: (identifier))))
  (repeat_statement label: (identifier)
    (body (undo_statement undo_label: (identifier) retry_label: (identifier)))))

UPDATE qty edit_loop~
: EDITING: READKEY. END.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
